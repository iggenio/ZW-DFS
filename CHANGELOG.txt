ZW-DFS - Changelog
================================================================
All notable changes to ZW-DFS are listed here, newest first.
Versions follow semantic versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH):
  MAJOR - incompatible changes (settings, file layout, hooks)
  MINOR - new features, backward compatible
  PATCH - bug fixes


[1.0.3] - 2026-11-04
----------------------------------------------------------------
Fixed
- The router Reboot button (top banner) did not work on the
  ZW-DFS page: the page did not load jQuery and httpApi.js, which
  the ASUS banner code needs ("$ is not defined"). jQuery is
  loaded from /js/jquery.js, with /jquery.js as fallback for
  firmware that keeps it in the web root.
- Updating the add-on could leave the old WebUI page in place
  (a new userN.asp slot was used for the new version). "install"
  now removes pages left by previous versions.
- All page functions now use the zw_ prefix, so they cannot clash
  with ASUS WebUI globals.


[1.0.2] - 2026-11-04
----------------------------------------------------------------
Fixed
- The Refresh button submitted the form like an apply, showing the
  router progress bar. It now only reloads the status, with no
  apply.

Changed
- The status is regenerated every minute (cron zwdfs_check, was
  every 5 minutes), so Refresh always shows recent data.


[1.0.1] - 2026-11-04
----------------------------------------------------------------
Fixed
- After a router reboot the move to the target failed: a full
  background radar check takes longer than after a recent use of
  the channel, and the move was wrongly considered finished after
  40 seconds. ZW-DFS now waits until the driver reports that the
  background check is over (up to 12 minutes).
- The fallback channel was refused by the driver because the
  previous background check was still running. ZW-DFS now waits
  for it to finish and retries once before giving up.
- After a failure without radar, the periodic check retries after
  10 minutes instead of 30 (30 minutes are kept after radar).

Added
- Log of the driver DFS state changes during the move, and the
  full driver status when a move does not complete (diagnostics).


[1.0.0] - 2026-11-04
----------------------------------------------------------------
First release. Tested on ASUS ROG Rapture GT-AXE11000 with
Asuswrt-Merlin 3004.388.12_2. Released under GPL-3.0-or-later.
Author: iggenio. Developed with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic).

Added
- Zero-wait DFS for the 5 GHz radio: start on the non-DFS channel
  saved in Wireless > General, then move to the target DFS channel
  with a background radar check (wl dfs_ap_move), without
  disconnecting clients.
- 90-second settle delay after the radio comes up, so clients are
  stable on the start channel before the move.
- Fallback DFS channel when radar blocks the target; radar events
  are logged to radar.log and shown in the WebUI.
- Automatic retry of the target channel after the 30-minute radar
  non-occupancy period (cron check every 5 minutes).
- Client tracking: clients on 5 GHz are counted before and after
  the move; clients that fall back to 2.4 GHz are sent back to
  5 GHz (one attempt per move) and reported by MAC and DHCP name.
- WebUI page under Wireless > ZW-DFS (English): status with
  synthetic move state (Idle, Waiting for clients to settle,
  Scanning, Moving, Checking clients, Recovering clients), last
  move summary, radar events, clients per band, recent events.
- Settings: automation on/off, target DFS channel, fallback DFS
  channel (channels in the target's 80 MHz block are not
  selectable).
- Buttons: Save, Move now, Refresh.
- Commands: install, startup, event, run [now], check, status,
  version, uninstall.
- Hooks: /jffs/scripts/service-event (Wi-Fi restart and WebUI
  actions) and /jffs/scripts/services-start (router boot).

Known limitations
- Radar detection is inferred from driver messages (dmesg); not yet
  verified with a real radar event.
- Requires a Broadcom driver with "wl dfs_ap_move"; other models
  or interface names may need changes (IF=eth7 in zwdfs.sh).
